Popular Places to Visit in Agra

Agra is known for its Mughal-era monuments, historic architecture and cultural heritage. Along with the Taj Mahal, visitors can explore several other attractions during an Agra sightseeing tour.

Photo: Taj Mahal

Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is an iconic white marble mausoleum situated on the banks of the Yamuna River in Agra. Built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of Mumtaz Mahal, it is recognised as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Photo: Agra Fort

Agra Fort

Agra Fort is a historic Mughal fortress known for its red sandstone architecture, royal structures and impressive courtyards. Located close to the Taj Mahal, it is another important attraction on an Agra heritage tour.

Photo: Baby Taj

Itimad-ud-Daulah (Baby Taj)

Popularly known as Baby Taj, Itimad-ud-Daulah is a Mughal-era mausoleum recognised for its detailed marble work and decorative architecture. It offers another glimpse into the architectural heritage of Agra.

Photo: Mehtab Bagh

Mehtab Bagh

Mehtab Bagh is a historic garden complex located across the Yamuna River from the Taj Mahal. It is known for its views of the monument and offers a different perspective of the Taj Mahal.

Frequently Asked Questions About Taj Mahal Tours

What is included in a Taj Mahal tour package?

The inclusions depend on the selected Taj Mahal tour package. Travellers should review the individual itinerary for details about transportation, accommodation, sightseeing, guide services and monument entry tickets.

How many days are enough for an Agra tour?

A same-day trip can cover major attractions such as the Taj Mahal and Agra Fort. An overnight or two-day Agra tour provides additional time to explore the city’s historical monuments and nearby attractions.

What is the best time to visit the Taj Mahal?

The early morning is a popular time to visit the Taj Mahal, particularly for its sunrise views and relatively cooler temperatures. October to March is generally a comfortable period for sightseeing in Agra.

Is the Taj Mahal open every day?

The Taj Mahal is open for general sightseeing from 30 minutes before sunrise until 30 minutes before sunset, except on Fridays when it remains closed to regular visitors. Check official visiting information before planning your trip.

Which attractions can be visited along with the Taj Mahal?

Visitors can explore Agra Fort, Itimad-ud-Daulah (Baby Taj) and Mehtab Bagh along with the Taj Mahal. Longer sightseeing itineraries may also include Fatehpur Sikri and Akbar’s Tomb.
